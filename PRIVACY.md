# Privacy Policy for Mini Crossword Date Navigator

Last updated: October 10, 2026

## Overview
Mini Crossword Date Navigator is an open-source browser extension designed to provide on-screen date navigation buttons on the New York Times Mini Crossword webpage.

## Data Collection
- **No Personal Data Collected**: The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing history, or user information.
- **No Third-Party Analytics**: The extension does not contain tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, or third-party cookies.
- **Local Operation**: All date arithmetic, DOM injection, and navigation occur entirely locally within your browser.

## Permissions Rationale
- **Host Permission (`https://www.nytimes.com/crosswords/game/mini*`)**: Required exclusively to inject the navigation buttons into the crossword header on NYT Mini puzzle pages. The extension has no access to other domains.

## Contact
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue in the project repository.
